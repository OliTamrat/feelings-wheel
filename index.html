How it works

From feeling to habit,
in four sittings.

01

Notice & name it precisely

“Mad” and “furious” need different responses. Drill centre → family → exact word.

02

See what it's associated with

Every deep dive shows triggers, body signals, inner voice, and the need underneath — so the feeling makes sense.

03

Write it down

Open My Notes from any feeling and put it into words — type, or dictate out loud. A named feeling, written down, is one you can act on.

04

Turn it into a habit

Send the feeling to the Habit Lab, pick one tiny habit, anchor it, and follow the 7-day plan.

02 · Deep dives

Every line, drilled down.

Twelve chapters — one for each core feeling. Open a chapter to find its four families, and under each family the specific feelings, every one with its own deep dive and its own habit. Tap any row.

03 · Habit Lab

One feeling.
One tiny habit.

Pick one feeling you want to work with. If it's a hard one, the Lab builds a habit that helps you get out of it faster. If it's a good one, it builds a habit that makes it happen more often. Small, specific, anchored to something you already do.

01

02

Three options matched to this feeling. Pick one — not all three.

03

Habits stick when they are attached to something you already do, and so small you can't say no.

04 · My Notes

Write it down —
feelings & goals.

An actionable pad, not a blank page. Write about a feeling you just named, or set a goal and the first small step toward it. Type, or tap the mic and talk. Everything saves as you go — on this device as a guest, or to your account when you're signed in — so it's still here when you come back.
